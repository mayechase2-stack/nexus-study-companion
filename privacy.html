NEXUS Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version

  • NEXUS accounts are for ages 13 and up. Kids under 13 may use NEXUS only with a parent or guardian, on the parent's own account, with the parent present. We do not allow children under 13 to create accounts.
  • Most of your study data lives in your own browser. If you create a cloud account, a copy is synced so you can switch devices.
  • When you ask the AI something, your question (and any image you attach or capture) goes through our secure server to OpenAI to create the answer. We don't keep a copy of your questions on our servers.
  • No ads. We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time in Settings → Data & History.

1. Who we are

NEXUS is an AI-powered study app available at nexusasc.com ("NEXUS," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information NEXUS collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions? Email support@nexusasc.com.

2. Age requirements (13+)

  • You must be 13 or older to create a NEXUS account, buy a plan, or use NEXUS on your own. We ask for a date of birth when an account is created (including when you sign in with Google for the first time) and block anyone under 13 from creating an account or purchasing.
  • Under 13: a child may use NEXUS only with a parent or legal guardian, on the parent's or guardian's own account, while the parent or guardian is present and supervising. The account belongs to the adult, and the adult is responsible for what is entered. Children should not type their own personal information (like their full name, address, school, or phone number) into NEXUS.
  • If you're between 13 and 17, a parent or guardian must agree to our Terms and make any purchase.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has created their own account, we will delete the account and its data. Parents who believe this has happened can contact support@nexusasc.com.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: a username, an email address (if you create a cloud account or sign in with Google), and your password. Passwords for cloud accounts are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as secure hashes — we never see your plain password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Date of birth: used only to confirm you meet the age requirement.
  • Your study content: the questions you ask, answers, notes, flashcards, practice results, study history, goals, and settings you create in NEXUS.
  • Images and screen captures you choose to send: photos you upload or paste, and — with Live Vision — frames of your screen or camera, captured only when you start sharing and only when you press a button (like "Solve This") to ask about them.
  • Files in your Class Library: notes and PDFs you add stay on your device (see "Stored on your device" below).
  • Feedback you send us through the in-app Feedback form.
  • Leaderboard (off unless you turn it on): if you join, your username and score are shown to other users.

Information collected automatically

  • AI usage and abuse prevention: when you use AI, we record a count of requests per account and per month, and the IP address the request came from, to enforce fair-use limits and stop automated abuse.
  • Sign-in session: an identifier for your current session and when it was last active, so an account is used on one device at a time.
  • Anonymous feature usage (only if you turn it on): if you switch on "Share anonymous usage stats" in Settings → Privacy (it's off by default), NEXUS sends which feature was opened and when — never your name, username, questions, or answers. A simple count of which features you use is also kept on your own device to power your study coach; it isn't sent to us.
  • Error reports: if the app hits a technical error, the error message, the page it happened on, and your browser type, so we can fix bugs.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends to any website (like IP address and browser type), which our hosting and service providers may log.

Stored on your device

NEXUS saves most of your data in your browser's storage (local storage and IndexedDB) so the app works quickly and offline: your progress, settings, history, Class Library files, and recent conversations. This stays on your device unless you use cloud sync. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your precise location, your contacts, payment card numbers (payments are handled by Stripe), or recordings of your voice or camera. We don't buy information about you from other companies.

4. How the AI works

  • When you ask a question, your prompt — plus any image you attach or capture, and short excerpts from your active Class Library notes if you've turned that on — is sent through NEXUS's secure server to OpenAI, which generates the answer. Inputs and outputs are checked by an automated safety filter.
  • NEXUS does not store your prompts or images on its servers. Under OpenAI's API data policies as of the date above, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models by default and may be kept by OpenAI for a limited time (currently up to 30 days) for abuse and misuse monitoring. See OpenAI's policies for details.
  • If you add your own OpenAI API key in Settings, it stays in your browser and your requests go directly to OpenAI under your own OpenAI account.
  • Please don't put personal information (full name, address, phone number, passwords, or anything private) into your questions or screen captures. AI answers can be wrong — always double-check important work.
  • Voice input: when you choose to speak (for example, hands-free lessons or "Say it out loud"), your browser's built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. In some browsers (like Chrome) the audio is processed by the browser maker's servers. NEXUS never records or stores audio.

5. How we use information

  • To provide NEXUS: answer questions, run lessons and practice, save and sync your progress, and remember your settings.
  • To keep NEXUS safe and fair: enforce usage limits, prevent abuse and fraud, and secure accounts.
  • To fix problems and improve features, using error reports and feature-usage counts.
  • To send account and service emails (for example, a welcome email, sign-in links, or important notices). We don't send marketing email without your permission.
  • To process payments once paid plans are available.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, show ads, or use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help run NEXUS, only for that purpose:

  • Supabase — accounts, cloud sync, our secure AI server, and data storage.
  • OpenAI — generating AI answers and safety checks (see section 4).
  • Stripe — payments, once paid plans launch. NEXUS never sees or stores your card number.
  • EmailJS — sending account emails (only the recipient's email address, username, and plan name are included).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — a privacy-friendly check that sign-ups are made by people, not bots.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the NEXUS website.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google."

Some content in NEXUS loads directly from other services when you use it, so your browser connects to them: Wikipedia/Wikimedia (photos in lessons and Social Studies), YouTube (the optional music player, only when you press play), and Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and cdnjs (fonts, icons, and code libraries).

We may also share information if required by law, to protect the safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger or sale of NEXUS — and in that case, we'll tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and export: Settings → Data & History → Export downloads a copy of everything stored on your device. For a copy of your cloud data, email us.
  • Correct: change your username and settings in the app, or email us.
  • Delete: Settings → Data & History → "Delete My Account & All Data" erases the data in your browser and, for a cloud account, deletes your synced data and the account itself. You can also email us from your account's email address. We complete deletion requests within 30 days. A small number of records that aren't linked to your account by name (such as rate-limit logs and error reports) are removed on the schedule in section 7.
  • Anonymous usage stats are off unless you turn them on (Settings → Privacy), and you can turn them off again any time.
  • The leaderboard is off by default. Turn it on or off any time (Settings → Study); turning it off removes you from the ranking.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us to review or delete information about their child by emailing support@nexusasc.com.

Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia, or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, such as the right to know, access, correct, delete, or port your information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We honor these requests for all users, wherever you live. Because we don't sell or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of, but we treat a browser's Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out request regardless. To make a request, email support@nexusasc.com; we may need to confirm it's really you before acting on it.

9. Teens and students

NEXUS is built for students in grade 9 and up. For users under 18, we never show ads, never sell data, and keep the leaderboard opt-in (it shows only a username and score). NEXUS has no public profiles and no messaging between users. NEXUS isn't operated on behalf of any school; if a school or teacher wants to use NEXUS with students, please contact us first so we can discuss a student data privacy agreement.

10. Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, per-account access rules in our database so only you can read your synced data, secure password hashing through our authentication provider, server-side storage of our AI keys, and owner-only access to error reports and feedback. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If a security incident affects your personal information, we'll notify you as required by law.

11. Where information is processed

NEXUS and its service providers process information in the United States and other countries where those providers operate. By using NEXUS from outside the U.S., you understand your information will be processed there.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app (Updates tab) at least 14 days before they take effect.

13. Contact us

Email support@nexusasc.com with any privacy question or request.
