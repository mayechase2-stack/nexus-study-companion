FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between Access and Pro?

Access ($4.50/mo) is the four core subjects only — Math, Science, Social Studies, English — with Tutor Mode and Live Vision. No companions, no Command Center, no shop. Pro ($6/mo) unlocks all of that plus Casual Mode (instant answers), companion personalities, the shop, achievements, the leaderboard, and the Smart Notebook. Switch plans anytime from Settings.

How does Tutor Mode actually teach?

Tutor Mode never just hands you the answer. It first explains the concept behind the problem — what's going on, why this kind of problem exists, what tool you'd reach for — then asks you to take the first step yourself. One step at a time, with feedback after each one. By the time you've worked through 2–3 problems together, the pattern actually sticks. Pro subscribers can flip to Casual Mode (instant answers) for quick checks.

What platforms does Live Vision work on?

All of them. Acellus, Edgenuity, Apex, FLVS, Khan Academy, IXL, Albert, Canvas, Schoology, Edmentum, ALEKS, WebAssign, Mastering Physics/Chem/Bio, MyMathLab — and anything else web-based. Live Vision reads what's on your screen, not the platform behind it, so as long as you can see the problem, NEXUS can read it.

Do I need my own OpenAI key?

No. Both Access and Pro include hosted AI — no key setup, no separate OpenAI bill, no surprise charges. Sign up, pay your subscription, use the app. (If you'd rather route through your own OpenAI account, you can flip a switch in Settings → Account & API.)

Can I cancel? What if I clear my browser?

Cancel anytime from Settings → Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period — no retention emails, no hoops.

What's the "backup file" you'll ask me to download?

NEXUS stores your account on your device, not our servers. Right after sign-up we prompt you to download a small .json file (under 100KB) containing your username, progress, credits, inventory, and equipped items — no password, no executable code. If you ever clear your browser data, switch devices, or reinstall your browser, that's the only way to restore — Settings → Data → Import loads it back. Keep it in your Documents folder, email it to yourself, or drop it in Google Drive / iCloud / Dropbox. Skip it and a browser-clear means your local progress is gone (your subscription itself still works, since that's billed separately).

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Simply enter a username and password on the login screen. Your data is saved locally on your device, so make sure to remember your credentials!

What can I do with NEXUS?

NEXUS offers AI-powered help for Math, Science, History, Social Studies, and English. You can solve problems, analyze concepts, take notes, and use interactive tools like the Math Visualizer and Science Simulator.

How do I navigate between sections?

Use the sidebar on the left to switch between Command Center (dashboard), subject-specific tools, and your Notebook. Each section has unique features tailored to that subject.

Shop & Credits Guide

How do I earn credits?

Earn credits by using the AI tools! Each time you solve a math problem, ask for help, or use any AI feature, you'll receive credits. Daily login rewards also grant bonus credits.

What can I buy in the shop?

The shop offers 6 categories: Themes (color schemes), Cursors (custom pointers), Fonts (text styles), Wallpapers (backgrounds), Badges (profile decorations), and Effects (visual flair).

What is Lucky Spin?

Lucky Spin is a gambling feature where you can spend credits for a chance to win rare or legendary items! Some items are only available through Lucky Spin. Cost: 500 credits per spin.

What do item rarities mean?

Items come in 4 rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Higher rarity items are harder to obtain and more exclusive!

Labs & Tools

What is the Math Visualizer?

The Math Visualizer graphs equations on a coordinate plane. Enter equations like y=x^2 or y=sin(x) to see them visualized. Find it in Command Center!

How do I use the Science Simulator?

The Science Simulator offers 3 interactive experiments: Pendulum Motion, pH Balance Testing, and Electric Circuits. Each shows formulas, concepts, and example problems to help you learn.

What is the History Timeline Builder?

Build custom timelines by adding historical events with years and descriptions. Or use pre-built templates for WWII, American History, or Ancient Civilizations to study major events.

How does the Notebook work?

The Notebook features realistic lined paper, auto-save, timestamp insertion, and a full drawing canvas! Use text formatting tools, draw diagrams, and everything saves automatically to localStorage.

Achievements & Secrets

How do achievements work?

Complete goals like solving problems, maintaining streaks, and collecting items to earn credit rewards. Check the Achievements tab to see your progress!

What are Nexus items?

Nexus items are secret legendary items hidden as easter eggs. Type "NEXUS" on your keyboard to discover them! Each discovery rewards 750 credits.

Can I customize my profile?

Yes! Click your profile picture in the Achievements tab to choose from 25 avatar options. Other users can view your profile on the Leaderboard!

Troubleshooting

My data is not saving!

NEXUS uses localStorage to save your data locally. Make sure your browser allows localStorage and you're not in incognito/private mode. Try clearing your browser cache if issues persist.

How do I reset my progress?

Open your browser's developer tools (F12), go to Application → Local Storage, and clear all NEXUS-related entries. This will reset your account, credits, and inventory.

The AI isn't responding to my questions

This app uses simulated AI responses for demonstration purposes. In a production version, this would connect to a real AI API like OpenAI or Anthropic's Claude.

Save a backup of your account

NEXUS stores your account on your device, not on our servers. Your username, progress, credits, inventory, and customization all live in this browser's storage.

WHAT THE BACKUP IS

A small .json text file (under 100KB) containing your account settings + progress. It is not malware and contains no executable code — it's just a list of your stats and equipped items. Your password is NOT stored in the file.

WHY YOU NEED IT

If you ever clear your browser data, switch devices, or reinstall your browser, your local progress is wiped. The backup is the only way to restore — Settings → Data → Import lets you load it back. Without one, your account becomes unrecoverable.

SAFE TO KEEP

Save the file to your Documents folder, email it to yourself, or drop it in a cloud drive (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox). Anywhere is fine — the file is small and harmless.

You can always grab one from Settings → Data → Export.
